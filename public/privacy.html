Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Makinote ("the app") is a personal note-taking app. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account info: if you create an account, we store your email address for sign-in purposes.
  • Your notes: titles, body text, checklists, tags, colors, and any voice recordings you create are stored so they can sync across your devices.
  • Nothing else: we don't collect analytics, tracking data, advertising identifiers, or sell any data to third parties.

Where your data lives

If you don't create an account, all your notes stay only on your device (local storage) and are never sent anywhere.

If you create an account, your notes and account info are stored using Supabase, a hosted database provider, so they can sync across your devices. Supabase acts solely as our data processor.

Sharing notes with other people

You can optionally connect your account with another user and share individual notes with them. Only notes you explicitly choose to share are visible to that connection — nothing is shared automatically.

Your control over your data

  • You can export all your notes at any time (Settings → Other → Export backup).
  • You can delete individual notes, or clear the app's local storage to remove everything from your device.
  • To delete your account and all associated cloud data, contact us at the email below.
